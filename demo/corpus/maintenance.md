# Maintenance Schedule

Routine maintenance keeps availability above 98 percent on most sites. The Fleet Manager tracks every interval below and opens a maintenance ticket when one is due.

## Daily

Operators check that every robot docked overnight and reached a full charge, and that no robot shows a red status on the dashboard. Clear debris from the lidar window and the depth camera lens with a dry microfibre cloth.

## Monthly

Inspect drive wheels for flat spots and embedded debris, and check caster wheels for free rotation. Vacuum the air inlets on the chassis sides. Verify that the deck lifts to its full height without noise.

## Every six months

Verify the safety fields with the test target as described on the Safety System page. Replace the drive wheel treads if the wear indicator is reached, which happens after roughly 3,000 km. Run a battery health report from the Operator Console; packs below 80 percent of their original capacity should be scheduled for replacement.

## Every two years

Replace the lidar window and the battery pack, and have a Halden service technician recalibrate the depth camera. Robots that miss this service lose their safety certification until it is performed.
